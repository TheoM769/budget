# Analytics

Answer the real question: where is the money going?

## What the user wants to do

Understand spending patterns. See how much came in vs. went out, which categories cost the most, and how habits evolve month to month. Examine a specific period — last month, last quarter, a holiday.

## How it works

- Launch with `analyze` from the home screen
- Loads all labeled transactions by default
- Filter by date range with `d` (interactive calendar picker)
- Clear filter with `r`

## What's shown

### Summary
Total income, total expenses, net balance, and transaction count for the selected period.

### Sankey Flow Diagram
ASCII visualization of income flowing into expense categories. Width and height of each band encode proportion. Savings shown as a separate band when income > expenses.

### Spending by Label
Bar chart of top 10 expense labels, sorted by amount. Immediate visual of the biggest cost drivers.

### Monthly Trend
Last 6 months: income, expenses, and net per month. Spot seasonal patterns or unusual months at a glance.

## Outcome

Clear, actionable picture of financial health over any chosen period.
